Returns & Refunds

Last updated: [DATE]

Your satisfaction is important to us. This policy outlines your rights regarding returns and refunds in accordance with EU consumer protection law.

1. Right of Withdrawal (14-Day Cooling-Off Period)

As a consumer in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from your purchase within 14 days without giving any reason, in accordance with the EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU.

The withdrawal period expires 14 days from the day you (or a person you designate, other than the carrier) acquire physical possession of the goods.

2. How to Return

To exercise your right of withdrawal:

  1. Contact us at info@infinytree.com with your order number and a statement that you wish to withdraw.
  2. We will confirm receipt and provide return instructions.
  3. Ship the product back within 14 days of informing us of your withdrawal, using the original packaging whenever possible.

3. Return Conditions

  • Products must be returned in original condition, unused and in their original packaging.
  • Due to the large size and fragile nature of our pieces, we strongly recommend using a professional shipping service with insurance.
  • You are liable for any diminished value resulting from handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature and characteristics of the product.

4. Return Shipping Costs

The direct cost of returning the goods is borne by you, the consumer. Given the size and weight of our products, please factor in the logistics of returning large items when placing your order.

5. Refund Process

  • Once we receive and inspect the returned product, we will process your refund.
  • Refunds are issued within 14 days of receiving the returned goods (or proof of return shipment).
  • We refund using the same payment method you used for the original purchase.
  • The refund includes the original delivery costs (standard shipping only — not supplementary costs for express or premium delivery options).

6. Damaged or Defective Products

If your product arrives damaged or defective:

  1. Document the damage with photographs immediately upon delivery.
  2. Contact us at info@infinytree.com within 48 hours with your order number and photos.
  3. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.

Under EU law, you are covered by a 2-year legal guarantee for defective goods.

7. Important Notes

  • Each Infinytree piece is handcrafted and unique. Natural variations in trunk shape, foliage distribution and dimensions are not considered defects.
  • Products used outdoors against our care recommendations are not eligible for warranty claims.
  • Product photographs on our website are representative samples. Minor visual differences do not constitute grounds for a defect claim.
