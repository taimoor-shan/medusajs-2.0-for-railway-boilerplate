Shipping to other EU countries may be available on request. Please contact us at info@infinytree.com.

2. Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. During peak periods, processing may take up to 5 business days. You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with tracking information.

4. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and order size. The final cost is displayed before you confirm your purchase.

5. Packaging

Every Infinytree piece is packed in custom protective packaging designed for large, delicate items. Some products are delivered in multiple parts with clear assembly instructions included.

6. Delivery Issues

If your package arrives damaged:

  1. Document the damage with photographs immediately.
  2. Note damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before signing.
  3. Contact us within 48 hours at info@infinytree.com.

For lost packages, contact us and we will investigate with the carrier promptly.
